# Security policy

Yhotos is internet facing and its source is public. Security reports are welcome and taken seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting on this repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability"). Do not open a public issue for anything that could be exploited.

Include what you can: affected component, steps to reproduce, impact, and any proof of concept. You will get an acknowledgement within 72 hours and updates as the fix progresses.

## Scope

In scope: anything in this repository, the default deployment described in the docs, and the official container images.

Out of scope: denial of service by volume alone, issues in third-party services (Cloudflare, Tailscale, GitHub), social engineering, and findings that require a compromised admin device.

## Supported versions

Pre-release. Only the `main` branch is supported until the first tagged release.

## Disclosure

Coordinated disclosure. Fixes are released before details are published, and reporters are credited unless they prefer not to be.
